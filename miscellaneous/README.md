# The Sovereign Barber Website

## Description
The Sovereign Barber is a modern barbershop committed to delivering premium grooming services. The website acts as a digital hub where customers can explore services, view the gallery, and book appointments.

## Features
- Home page
- Bookings page
- About Us
- Services
- Gallery
- Contact Us

## Pages / Structure
- **Home**
  - About Us (Our story)
    - Mission and Values
    - Meet Our Barber
  - Services
    - Haircuts
    - Styling
    - Beard trimming
  - Bookings
    - Select time and date
  - Gallery
    - Hairstyles
    - Beardwork
  - Contact Us
    - Contact form
    - Phone and Email
    - Social links
- **Bookings**: appointment selection
- **Gallery**: image showcase
- **Contact**: contact details + social links

## Notes
- The navbar layout is styled in `sovereign website/style.css` to keep the logo and navigation links aligned.
